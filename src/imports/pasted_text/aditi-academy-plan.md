Project Goal: Transform ADITI Academy's online presence into a highly professional, authoritative platform (following the EC-Council style) to effectively attract and convert B2B/Corporate, B2G/Government, and individual professional clients. 

1. WHAT? (Scope & Deliverables) 

The core deliverable is a complete set of high-fidelity mockups and detailed user flows for a modern, segmented professional academy website. 

Component 

Requirement 

Project Scope 

Design a website that clearly showcases our complete offering: information, past work, current projects, and accomplishments in collaboration with local and international partners. 

Core Offering 

Deliver training for: University Students, Government Staff, and Corporate Professionals (IT upskilling ranging from development to cybersecurity).  

Functional Priority 

The design must be certification-centric and structured around clear, high-value Career Tracks (like EC-Council). 

Key Metric 

The design must drastically improve the clarity and usability of the course catalog and the lead-generation forms for corporate inquiries.
